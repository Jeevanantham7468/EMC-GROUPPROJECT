How are key collector comics shipped?

All single issues ship bagged & boarded inside rigid Gemini comic mailers with reinforced corner protection.

What grading standards are followed?

We strictly evaluate condition in accordance with the Overstreet Comic Book Grading standard.

How does the Monthly Pull List work?

We automatically reserve selected issues upon release and dispatch them bundled every month with zero missed issues.
